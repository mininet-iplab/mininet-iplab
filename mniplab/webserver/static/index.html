Mininet-IPLab

Mininet-IPLab is a network emulation framework for teaching and testing IP routing protocols. It extends Mininet with FRR routing daemons, ExaBGP speakers, and a web UI for topology visualization and in-browser terminals.

            Help & Keyboard Shortcuts

            Getting Started

            • Open Terminal: Click on any node in the topology or sidebar to open a terminal. A Switch has no terminal: click it to open its Switch panel.
            • Move Nodes: Drag nodes to rearrange the topology view, or focus a node and use the Arrow keys to nudge it (see Keyboard Shortcuts).
            • Pan & Zoom: Click and drag empty space to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom, or use the zoom buttons in the topology's bottom-right corner (see Layout & Organization).

            Layout & Organization

            • Auto-Arrange Nodes (bottom-right corner): Unpins every node, lets the physics re-arrange them, then re-centres the view. Discards your Personal Layout, and cannot be undone.
            • Zoom In / Zoom Out (bottom-right corner): Zoom the topology in or out by a fixed step, for users without pinch or scroll. The same controls the wheel and pinch gestures drive.
            • Fit (bottom-right corner): Centers the topology without moving any nodes.
            • Reset to Authored Layout: Resets the canvas to this lab's authored layout, discarding your own drags.
            • Grouping: Select multiple nodes (Ctrl+Click) then choose 'Group' under View to create a visual cluster. With a cluster selected, 'Ungroup' appears there too.
            • Link Configuration: Click a link in the topology to open link actions (disconnect/reconnect) and settings.

            Troubleshooting

            Reset this browser’s saved theme, panel sizes and other Mininet-IPLab preferences. The page reloads; saved Labs, drafts and configurations are kept.
